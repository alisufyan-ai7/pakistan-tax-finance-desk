# Possible future product: exploration only

## Binding decision

**Build and operate as a private single-owner application first.** No current commitment to monetize, multi-tenant accounts, marketing spend or regulated advice to the public. Reconsider commercialization only after personal workflows reliably work and data protection is mature.

## Potential audience (hypotheses; validate, not market facts)

1. Pakistan salaried individuals with several bank accounts and investments needing IRIS-ready reconciliation.
2. Freelancers, remote workers, independent consultants and foreign-remittance recipients with complex bank flows.
3. Sole proprietors / small business owners mixing personal and business cash flows and wanting auditability.
4. Investors with brokerage statements, mutual funds, bank certificates, real estate and loans to relatives/friends.
5. Tax consultants/accounting firms (a later professional workspace with client permissions, reviewer sign-off and no unauthorized client data exposure).
6. Overseas Pakistanis managing local assets and tax obligations (residency-specific; later).

## Differentiation candidates

Pakistan-first tax-year historical engine; bank-statement ingestion; 2023+ full wealth and liability graph; FBR wealth reconciliation; bilingual possible future interface; explainability and evidenced tax estimates; low effort; planning scenarios; privacy and user-controlled export.

## Possible monetization if later approved

Free limited statement analytics; annual tax-year preparation package; monthly individual financial planning dashboards; per-client accountant workspace; optional certified tax-professional review as a separate regulated/contractual service. These are ideas, not pricing decisions.

## Stage gates before any public launch

- Validate who pays and which problem matters using consenting testers and synthetic/test data.
- Obtain Pakistan tax/legal advice for software preparation disclaimers, duties and data processing; confirm SECP boundaries before offering personalized investment advice or brokerage-related suggestions.
- Build tenant isolation/permission architecture, account lifecycle, consent, data deletion/export, incident response, secure payment, support, abuse prevention and audit.
- Assess bank aggregation/API rights, external market-data commercialization rights, PDF intellectual property and FBR authorized filing integrations.
- Obtain independent security review and test restore/reliability; build human tax quality assurance and support response.

## Avoid premature lock-in

Keep clean owner/entity and date-effective data schema; plug-in bank parser framework; separated rule engine and advisory interface; provider-agnostic encrypted file storage and deployment; no customer-facing plan hardcoded into model names or tables. Single-user simplicity remains the priority.
