## Staging and release acceptance

- **Private synthetic demo:** can be exercised with fabricated records, never advertise real-bank or official-tax readiness.
- **Private owner alpha with real data:** requires G1, G2 and at least one fully verified G3 input path, authorized owner approval, encrypted originals and actual backup recovery checks; limited supported formats must be explicit. Tax and adviser features with unverified legal treatments remain marked `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`/`null`.
- **Owner MVP usable for annual FBR work:** requires G1–G5 for the owner's actual sources, G4 independent legal computations and full wealth reconciliation, plus reliable annual import and private browser testing. Human approval of any filing; no unsupported autonomous IRIS submission.
- **Public multi-user launch:** never inferred from owner MVP success; needs separate G6.

## Engineering execution rules

1. Finish at least one concrete, owner-independent gate at a time; preserve modular CFO scope without introducing unused microservices or premium services. Link each PR to its gate, tests and risks.
2. Build and test real-format **adapters with synthetic variants now**. Request the genuine documents only when private intake exists and actual acceptance needs them.
3. Never certify a security gate from mere encryption of a second copy, or a tax gate from a hypothetical salary-slab preview. Verify the active read/write path and legally relevant source facts.
4. Keep a single canonical [project status](PROJECT_STATUS.md) and [handoff](CURRENT_HANDOFF.md) with latest validated SHA and exceptions. Follow [mandatory continuity protocol](CHAT_CONTINUITY_PROTOCOL.md) before a new chat or thread ends.

Future owner documents and infrastructure consent are acceptance inputs, not blockers for the present synthetic engineering.
