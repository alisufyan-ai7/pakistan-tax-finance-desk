# Questions to resolve at the appropriate stage (nonblocking)

Do **not** delay documentation or synthetic engineering MVP for these. Ask together in a small one-time onboarding flow only when required.

1. Which Pakistani and overseas banks/wallets, accounts and statement formats? Which are personal vs business/company owned?
2. Do historic records begin before Jan 2023? What was the opening cash, loans, assets, investments and acquisition cost basis on the chosen start date?
3. Are prior FBR returns/wealth statements accessible to reconcile opening wealth and filed figures? Any notices or pending proceedings?
4. Resident/nonresident status by tax year; filing status, income heads, salary gross/withholding and business legal entity types?
5. Which investment classes: PSX, brokerage, mutual funds, National Savings, gold, real estate, crypto or others? Are broker exports available?
6. Any family gifts, donations, zakat, charitable receipts, loans/credit cards, assets held jointly, personal remittances or foreign-currency balances?
7. For advice only: short/long goals, reserves, major planned cash needs, risk tolerance, debt interest, investment horizon, currency and optional Shariah constraints.
8. Deployment preference: private local machine vs managed cloud; encryption/key backup and permissible AI provider data sharing.
9. If commercialization is later approved: target audience, regulatory review, tenant and privacy controls, support/price model.

## Provisional working assumptions

PKR reporting currency, `Asia/Karachi` UI timezone, personal income tax first, historic TY2023+ normal Pakistan tax years, no direct broker trading and no FBR credential storage. Every assumption is visible and overridable.
